# Cabin World 1.2 · Current public build

- **Player appearance.** The settings screen shows portraits of Tuantuan and Little Whale. Select a card to change the player in both worlds. A new save starts as Tuantuan. A custom transparent PNG or a four-row directional walking sheet can also be imported.
- **Village water audio.** Walking into the shallow canal plays a splash on the first moving step. Continued movement varies the sound. Standing still produces no footsteps, and stone bridges use stone footsteps.
- **Farm and kitchen.** Players and residents can plant seven crops. Bank-side fields take priority when selected; wheat has visible growth stages. The farmhouse stove displays twenty recipes under meat dishes, vegetables, soups and staples. Strawberries and watermelon can be eaten directly. Livestock can roam farther across accessible pasture.
- **Arcade.** Players sit on the assigned chess stool and face their opponent. Background music levels in the arcade, park and village center have been balanced across day and night.
- **Editable content.** The editable directory contains gameplay scripts, scenes, shaders and configuration. Artwork and audio can be exported and replaced with the included offline resource tools. The local bridge and examples are included for connecting a resident with your own configuration.
- **Fresh start.** The Windows archive runs independently. A new player's farm starts empty, with a single-story barn. The public edition includes pet food and the resident Little Whale.

See the accompanying English release guide for the comparison with version 1.1 and the Chinese editing guide for exact file paths.


## Shared farm storage and resident decisions

Place a shared chest, press E, and choose an item and quantity. Residents can approach it through the local bridge and withdraw seeds for planting. The public bridge exposes editable budgeting guidance and an optional group-turn routing helper. Chat adapters supply their own group events, mentions and quoted authors. Shared storage follows the Qingxi Village selection. Budgeting and group-turn guidance are independent choices.

The planting menu shows all seven crops in a four-column icon grid. Each choice shows the available seed count. Out-of-stock crops are dimmed.
